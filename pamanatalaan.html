Katangian · Ability Scores

At Antas 1, place a D10, D8, D6, and D4, one die each. After that, spend your Ability Pool by stepping scores up through the tiers; every step costs D2 from the pool. To make a check, roll 1D20 and add the matching ability die.

Ability Pool D0 placed of D28
At Antas 1, each score takes a different die: D10, D8, D6, and D4.

Husay · Skills

Fill six open slots, Bayani: 1 Passive, 1 Lakas, 1 Linay, 1 Listo, 1 Liksi, and 1 Utility/Buff. Once your Pamana and ability dice are set, each slot offers only the Husay your dice can reach. Changing your Pamana or Ability Focus clears every slot; changing an ability die clears the slot that die governs.

Kagamitan at Tala · Equipment & Notes

Habi, to weave: the Balatik is an incomplete tapestry of the Pamana still remembered. Touch a weave to dress it, and touch it again to read its story.

Choose a tile in the tapestry, Bayani, and its four abilities will open before you: Lakas, Linay, Listo, and Liksi, each carrying its own Tadhana.
Pamana · Core Systems Reference

Anyo Action Economy SystemTM

The round is woven in three bands, Bayani. Touch an Anyo to open its choices, then touch a choice to learn what it does.

Lakas Attack Husay Movement (60 ft) or Movement Husay Reaction (held Lihim na Anyo)

Each turn moves through three Anyo in order. Unang Anyo opens with a Lakas attack, any Husay, or a Movement Husay. Pangalawang Anyo is for moving only: a plain Movement of up to 60 feet, or a Movement Husay. Lihim na Anyo takes a Husay, or you may hold it as a Reaction until your next turn. Every roll uses the die and ability score the chosen Husay calls for.

Touch any Anyo in the tapestry to open its choices, then touch a choice to read its description here.
